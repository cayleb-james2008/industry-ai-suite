# Industry AI Suite — project case study

Industry AI Suite puts ten local review tools on one Python foundation. Each tool prepares a bounded result with its source evidence, uncertainty, and next step for a human reviewer.

The workbench supports read-only public-source reviews and local organization-bundle checks. It records citations and minimized review history, and lets a reviewer export or delete a receipt. Organization-bundle processing is deterministic and runs on the user's machine; safe examples contain invented data. The optional model path is loopback-only.

The merged implementation passed 382 tests, 178 subtests, and 19 browser checks on 13 October 2026. A separate witness bundle records six local model exchanges; five produced cited output accepted by the app validator and checked against public sources. These results cover bounded tasks, not complete company workflows.

All ten full organization workflows remain `UNVERIFIED` until their private records, source permissions, and responsible owners are available. The importer does not authenticate the submitter or verify that a bundle is complete. The suite cannot send, publish, trade, or change accounts, and no deployment, customer use, revenue, or commercial result is claimed.
