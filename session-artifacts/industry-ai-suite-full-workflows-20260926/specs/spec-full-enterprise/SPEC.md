# Ten enterprise jobs: implementation and honest verification

## Why

Cayleb approved the published dark portfolio and wants the ten Industry AI Suite products to work beyond bounded public-data demonstrations. A prospective employer should be able to inspect a real local workflow and see what input, decision, evidence, and human handoff each product supports. No real company records or credentials were supplied with this request.

## Capabilities

- **CAP-1**
  - **intent:** Each named product has a distinct, bounded end-to-end review path for its intended enterprise records.
  - **success:** Inputs are validated before work, sources and limits are shown, and each result provides a usable decision or draft plus a named human next action. Pure domain checks from existing apps are reused where applicable.
- **CAP-2**
  - **intent:** Local user-supplied inputs work without fixture or public-data substitution.
  - **success:** Each product has an import contract and rejection path. The workbench stays loopback-only, caps inputs, does not persist private payloads or send external messages, and labels organizational authority as unverified when it is only asserted.
- **CAP-3**
  - **intent:** AI helps a review only when its contribution is evidence-bound and truthful.
  - **success:** A configured free local route can provide a bounded cited candidate without sending raw private records to a hosted provider. Independent witnessing remains separate; deterministic output is never labeled AI.
- **CAP-4**
  - **intent:** Every journey shows evidence, uncertainty, and a responsible human decision point.
  - **success:** The result exposes evidence IDs, input hash or source provenance, failure states, human review, and a downloadable receipt. No sending, posting, containment, trade, transfer, or account changes occur in this run.
- **CAP-5**
  - **intent:** Public presentation remains truthful and preserves Cayleb's approved design.
  - **success:** The dark portfolio, ten recorded receipts, and five witnessed cited samples retain their evidence meaning. Public copy keeps full enterprise jobs `UNVERIFIED` until real authorized organization sources and complete journeys are independently checked.

## Completion rule

Two verdicts are separate. **Engineering verified** means a safe representative import traverses UI and backend with passing adversarial, accessibility, and security checks. **Enterprise verified** requires actual authorized records, organizational identity and policy, permissions, and observed human decision or allowed integration for the exact job. A fixture or a self-attested manifest cannot satisfy the latter. If those inputs are absent, report the exact blocker per product while shipping independently useful engineering.

## Constraints

- Inherit suite `770d541d09c7c576c7a5aea53706842d12623549` and portfolio `1de40ae42e89cbf8ab7b7490f77a61195c1130ca`; preserve their evidence and approved appearance.
- One local request is bounded to 128 KB, one chosen workflow, explicit record schemas, and no disk write of raw user payloads. Narrower limits may apply per app.
- No unapproved use of private records, credentials, paid services, external messaging, publication of private inputs, or action authority.
- Security review must test malformed schemas, duplicate IDs, cross-tenant or access claims, prompt injection, sensitive-field leakage, replay or forged approvals where relevant, and unavailable sources.
- Every implemented path has meaningful tests and a real browser journey; full test suite and live release are checked after repair. Tests may use clearly labelled safe fixtures only for engineering verification.

## Non-goals

- No invented customer/company deployment, productivity result, revenue, consent, employee record, or approval.
- No speculative integration with a named CRM, HRIS, SIEM, ledger, broker, wallet, or identity provider before the organization and its allowed APIs are known.
- No applications or external messages.

## Success signal

All ten products pass meaningful local import, decision, denial, and browser checks against clearly labeled safe representative input while preserving the approved site. The final report identifies exactly which enterprise jobs remain `UNVERIFIED` and what genuine organizational evidence is still needed.
